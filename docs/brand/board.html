Headroom · brand board · September 2026

Which name, and which mark?

Headroom answers one question — which AI account still has room right now — in a category that names itself like spare parts: ClaudeBar, CodexBar, ClaudeMeter, TokenBar. The name is also taken three times over in this exact niche, including a paid menu-bar app aimed at the same people. Whatever it ends up called, the brand's job is reassurance: one calm ring, numbers first, green as the default mood.

Ten concepts, each worked to the same depth so they can be compared honestly — the mark at icon and menu-bar size, the wordmark sitting in the panel header, and the voice in a notification. Star the ones worth another round; notes stay in this browser. The neighbourhood — fifteen trackers and tools, what they are called and what they cost — sits below the scorecard.

How the category names itself
The candidates

NamesAlmost everyone is Something-Bar, -Meter, -Tracker or -Usage — a category label, not a brand — and the real-word exceptions all took “Headroom”. A real word nobody else has is the open lane; it is also why Headroom no longer reads as ours.

PriceFree and MIT is the default, funded by sponsorship. The one paid tracker sells one-time licences at $7–59 and gates multi-account, history and widgets behind Pro. Subscriptions only appear where the tool saves money. If this ever charged, a one-time $9–15 is the norm; nobody pays monthly to look at a ring.

Table stakesSession and weekly windows, a reset countdown, threshold notifications, a percentage in the menu bar, and “reads the sign-ins you already have”. Everyone has these.

Where others go furtherProvider breadth (CodexBar’s 69), a CLI or local API for scripts (CodexBar, OpenUsage), the Claude Code statusline (Claude Tracker, ccusage), widgets and companions (CodexBar, SessionWatcher Pro, Zappa’s iPhone and Watch), cost and token analytics (TokenBar, ccusage, SessionWatcher), incident badges (CodexBar), a notch HUD (Claude Tracker).

Where Headroom is already aheadSeveral accounts per provider with in-app sign-in and no cookie pasting — only SessionWatcher Pro and Claude Tracker do multi-account, and neither signs you in. Plan and monthly cost per account with a total; nobody else shows what the plans cost. Quiet hours and reset-after-low alerts. A designed interface, where the field is stock SwiftUI or Liquid Glass.

Gaps if it goes publicSix providers against nine or more, no statusline, no widgets, no CLI, no Windows.
